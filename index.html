AI is too complicated for my team. Can we actually do this in-house? +

That's exactly why we exist. We don't hand you a tool and walk away — we train your team hands-on, build the workflows around the way you already work, and leave you with playbooks your staff can follow without any technical background. If your team can use email, they can run these systems.

I already tried ChatGPT and it didn't work for my business. +

ChatGPT is a tool. We train you to build the workflow, the prompting architecture, and the business-specific context wrapped around it. Most people who "tried AI and it didn't work" were using a general-purpose tool for a specific-purpose problem without the right setup. That's what we fix.

It depends on the tier, but the math tends to work in your favor fast. If a workflow saves your team even 10 hours a month at $50/hour, that's $500/month recouped. We believe your AI infrastructure should be as core to your business as your CRM or email. We walk you through setting it up yourself, on your own accounts, step by step, so you aren't beholden to a third-party agency to keep it running.
